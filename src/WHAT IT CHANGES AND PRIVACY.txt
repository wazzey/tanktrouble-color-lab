TANKTROUBLE COLOR LAB - WHAT IT CHANGES AND PRIVACY
Not affiliated with TankTrouble. A fan-made, cosmetic-only extension.

Color Lab is cosmetic only. It gives no advantage in the game: no aim help, no
seeing through walls, no speed or reload changes. Everything below is visual.
Only you and other Color Lab users see it (see LOOK SHARING), unless you choose
to save a real change to your tank.


WHAT IT CHANGES ON YOUR SCREEN
- Tank colours: turret, base and treads of your tanks and of any tank you
  give a custom look.
- Paints and items: extra pattern paints and accessories (hats, glasses,
  capes, auras and so on), in their own boxes in the garage.
- Garage: adds folders (Original / Custom) and Color Lab boxes to the shelf,
  plus a search panel.
- Maps: the maze floor, walls, crates and decorations can use a map theme.
- Page themes: the page's background, header, side boxes, the game's buttons,
  the lobby pictures, the garage and the panel can take a theme's colours
  on every page, forum, news and shop included (forum threads and replies
  can follow it, go dark or stay normal).
- Bullets: bullet colours can match the map, and multi-coloured bullets spin.
  The laser and aiming lines, mines and mine shrapnel are recoloured so they
  stay visible on every map.
- Laika (the computer opponent): Laika's tank and Laika's dog can dress up to
  match the map.
- Achievements: an extra Achievements tab with hidden achievements that
  unlock Color Lab items and paints. They are separate from the game's own.
- A panel (Alt+C), a short tutorial and update notes.


WHAT OTHER PLAYERS SEE
Players without Color Lab see the normal game. Color Lab looks are never sent
to the game's server.
Players with Color Lab see your Color Lab look too (see LOOK SHARING).
The only exception is the garage's "Real" mode (and the dice / "Find in Real"
buttons). These change your actual tank on your account, exactly like the
game's own garage, and only with items you already own.


WHAT IT CAN ACCESS
- It runs only on tanktrouble.com. It does not run on any other website and
  cannot see your other tabs, history, cookies, passwords or downloads.
- It also talks to Color Lab's look server (ttcl-share.wazzey.workers.dev) for
  look sharing, and to nothing else.
- Browser permissions it asks for: "storage" (to save your looks) and
  "scripting" (so the toolbar button / Alt+C can open the panel).
- It reads TankTrouble's own game messages in the page, so it can recolour
  tanks and notice kills, wins and pickups for achievements. Your login
  passes through the game the normal way. Color Lab does not read, keep
  or send your password.


WHAT IT STORES (only on your own computer)
- Your saved looks, presets, favourites and settings.
- For tanks you give a custom look: their in-game username, player ID and
  any nickname you type.
- Achievement progress: your kills, wins, streaks and which days you played.
  The time of day is checked for one achievement and is not saved.
- A backup copy of the above in TankTrouble's site storage in your browser,
  so your looks survive reinstalling the extension.
- A cache of pictures so the garage loads faster.

LOOK SHARING
So that Color Lab users can see each other's looks, Color Lab's look server
(run by the author on Cloudflare) keeps:
- for each of your logged-in tanks that has a Color Lab look: its player ID
  and that look (paints, items, which patterns show in the maze, the bullets
  you picked), and when it was last saved;
- a scrambled copy (hash) of a random key made by your copy of Color Lab, so
  only your copy can change or remove your tank's shared look.
To show other tanks' looks, your copy sends the server the player IDs of the
tanks around you, and keeps a connection open so changes arrive at once.
Nothing else is sent: no passwords, no stats, no browsing. So the author can
deal with abuse, the server notes each shared tank's TankTrouble name (public
in the game) next to its look.
The server keeps no record of who asked for which looks. Like any website,
Cloudflare (which runs it) sees your IP address when your browser connects.
To stop people sharing looks for other players' tanks, a look is only shared
once you prove the tank is yours: Color Lab changes one thing on your real
tank for about a second (a paint you already wear moved to another part, or
one item taken off), the server reads the tank's public details on
TankTrouble to see the change, and your tank is put straight back. Once per
tank, and Color Lab asks first. Looks can be reported from a tank's profile.
Also, a copy of Color Lab
(and an internet connection) can only share a few new tanks a day. For that
the server keeps a scrambled one-way code made from your connection, for one
day, never stored next to a look. A banned connection's code is kept
until the ban is lifted.
Switch it off in Settings > Look sharing:
- "Share my look" off removes your logged-in tanks' looks from the server.
- "See other players' looks" off stops asking for anyone's look.

ONLINE STATUS
Every few minutes (and when you log in or out, or go idle) your copy also tells
the look server that it is running, so the author can see how many copies are
in use and which version they are:
- a scrambled copy (hash) of your copy's random key, its version, and when it
  was first and last seen;
- which of your tanks are logged in right now (player IDs, guests included),
  which TankTrouble server you are playing on (for example "London"), and
  whether you are idle (the TankTrouble tab is hidden, or nothing has been
  clicked or typed for 5 minutes) or away (the tab has been in the background
  for 3 minutes while you are in the lobby), and whether you are in the lobby,
  a queue or a game.
The author can see all of this. Other Color Lab players see you in the Color Lab
box on the page ONLY if you turn on Settings > Look sharing > "Show me as
online": then they see your name and whether you are online or idle (and in
the lobby, a queue or a game), only for
tanks proven to be yours (never guests). Your server (for example "London") is
shown only if you also turn on "Show which server I'm on". Otherwise you are
only counted as "hidden". It is kept for up to 60 days after your copy was
last seen (the logged-in tanks for 30), then deleted. Switching off
Settings > Look sharing > "Share my look" stops the tanks, server and idle
status from being sent and hides you; the key hash and version are still sent
so replies to your messages can reach you. Away, or idle for 15 minutes or
more, shows as offline to other players. In an Incognito window Color Lab never
contacts its server at all.

MESSAGES
If you send a message from Settings > Feedback & reports, its text and any
screenshots go to the author's Discord, with your tank's name. The text and
replies are kept so you can see them in Color Lab; screenshots are not kept.

UPDATE CHECK
Now and then Color Lab asks the look server which version is the newest (the
server asks GitHub, at most once an hour), and tells you when there's a new
one. Nothing about you is sent. Switch it off in Settings > Help.

There is no tracking, no analytics and no account.

Apart from the look server, it only talks to TankTrouble itself.


DELETING EVERYTHING
1. Settings > Export first, if you want to keep a copy of your looks.
2. Remove the extension at chrome://extensions.
3. Clear tanktrouble.com's site data (click the padlock next to the address,
   then Site settings > Delete data). This removes the backup copy and
   picture cache.
To also remove your shared look from the look server, switch off
Settings > Look sharing > Share my look BEFORE removing the extension.


STAY SAFE
Only install Color Lab from the official download:
https://github.com/wazzey/tanktrouble-color-lab/releases
Check the zip's SHA-256 matches the one listed there. A changed copy from
someone else could do things this one does not.
