# TankTrouble Color Lab

Made and run by **wazzey**. **Not affiliated with TankTrouble.** Color Lab is an independent, fan-made, cosmetic-only browser extension. TankTrouble, its name and its artwork belong to their owners.

Color Lab lets you reskin TankTrouble your way: custom colours and pattern paints for your tank, extra accessories, themed maps, matching bullets and crates, and new looks for Laika. It is **purely cosmetic**. It gives no advantage in the game. Only **you and other Color Lab users** see your looks. Everyone else sees the normal game.

## About this project

This started as a personal project. I just wanted to see how different looks would work in the game. Once it grew, I decided to make it public so others can enjoy it too.

- It is **free and not for profit**. There is nothing to buy and no ads.
- I work on it in my **spare time**, so updates and changes come whenever I'm free.
- **AI was used** to help make this, including much of the artwork for the paints and accessories, so don't expect every picture or feature to be perfect.
- **Feedback is very welcome**: ideas, bugs, or anything I could do better. That includes artwork: if a paint or accessory doesn't sit well on the actual tank, or a pattern doesn't look right in the in-game maze, let me know. Reply on the [forum thread](https://tanktrouble.com/forum?id=475180) or open an [issue](https://github.com/wazzey/tanktrouble-color-lab/issues) here.

## Main features

**🎨 Colour panel (Alt+C)**
Pick any tank by name (or press **Me** for your own), then paint the turret, base and treads separately. Use any hex colour, a pattern paint, or 🎲 for a random one. Pattern paints can show in the maze too. **Swatches** lists every box's paints and items, so you can try anything on.

**💾 Saved looks and presets**
**Save** keeps a look for that tank. Each tank can have up to 10 **presets** to switch between, and each saved tank can be switched on or off. Back everything up with **Settings > Export**.

**🔁 Your real look stays safe**
Your Color Lab look never changes your real TankTrouble look, the one players without Color Lab see. To change your real look, switch to **Real** in the garage (or pause Color Lab with ⏻), change it as normal, then switch back to **All** or turn ⏻ back on. Your custom look comes straight back, and your new real look is what everyone else sees.

**🏠 In-game garage**
In the game's own garage, **All** shows every paint and item, plus Color Lab's boxes in folders (Original / Custom, sorted by genre). **Real** is your normal garage and saves to your account. There's also a 🎲 random look button and a **Find in garage** search.

**🗺 Map themes**
75 themes for the maze floor, walls and crates, each light or dark, with matching bullets. Pick one, take turns, or get a random one every round. Laika dresses up to match.

**🎨 Page themes**
Theme the TankTrouble page itself in any map's colours: the background, header and its tabs, side boxes, the game's buttons, the lobby, the garage, the forum, news and shop pages, and the Color Lab panel. There are 74, plus 🎃 **Halloween**, 🎄 **Christmas** and 🐣 **Easter**. Match the map you're playing on, keep one, or get a new one every day (the seasonal ones come round on their dates). The maze and Laika can use it too. Forum threads and replies can follow the theme, go dark (the rest of the page keeps the theme), or stay as normal. Every theme's writing is checked to stay easy to read. Find it in the 🗺 Maze tab.

**🔔 Update check**
When a new version of Color Lab is out, it tells you what's new and how to update. You can check any time in **Settings > Help**, or switch it off there.

**🔗 Look sharing**
Color Lab users see each other's looks, in the lobby, the garage and the maze. When someone saves a new look, it pops in for everyone straight away. Click a tank with a Color Lab look and its profile has a **Real / Color Lab** switch. Only your tank's player ID and look are shared, and you can switch sharing off in **Settings > Look sharing**.

**🟢 Who's online**
A Color Lab box in the lobby shows how many Color Lab players are online. Names are opt-in: turn on **Show me as online** (and **Show which server I'm on**) in **Settings > Look sharing**. Everyone else is only counted as hidden.

**💬 Message Color Lab**
A box on the right of the lobby, laid out like the game's message laboratory. Write to me with ideas or bugs, add up to 3 screenshots, and read the replies there.

**⚙ Color Lab in the game's settings box**
The game's own ⚙ box (top right) has Color Lab's quick controls: on/off, sharing, going offline, your page theme, map, Laika and your bullets picked from pictures, plus the version, update notes and a check for updates.

**👁 Easy to see on every map**
Mines, homing missiles, lasers and aim lines whose colour is too close to the floor (like a black turret on a dark map) get a colour that stands out, and bullets get an edge where they would blend in. Picking a bullet colour that's hard to see shows a warning.

**📢 Announcements**
News from me pops up in the top right corner the moment I send it, without interrupting your game (it closes by itself after 30 seconds). Every announcement is also kept in **Message Color Lab > 📢 Announcements**.

**🔌 Go offline**
One switch stops Color Lab sharing anything with its server (no look sharing, no online list, no messages) until you switch it back on. Only the update check still asks which version is newest, which sends nothing about you. Everything on your own screen keeps working.

**🏆 Hidden achievements**
Color Lab's own achievements, earned in online rounds. Each one unlocks an item, and the hardest also unlock a paint.

## Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera)

1. Unzip it and keep the `TankTrouble-Color-Lab` folder somewhere safe. The browser runs it from there.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`, `brave://extensions`).
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right in Chrome, Brave and Opera; in the left-hand menu in Edge).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the `TankTrouble-Color-Lab` folder.
5. Pin it from the puzzle-piece icon, then open or refresh tanktrouble.com and press **Alt+C**.

**First time?** A short built-in tutorial starts on its own. Choose a guided tour of either the **colour panel** or the **in-game garage** (you can take the other one after). Replay it any time from a **?** button or **Settings > Tutorial**.

Your browser may warn about extensions in Developer mode. That's normal for anything installed this way.

**Updating:** copy the new files over your old folder, press reload on the extension, then refresh TankTrouble. Your looks are kept.

**Getting told about updates:** click **Watch → Custom → Releases** at the top of this page (you need a free GitHub account), or keep an eye on the forum thread. Each release lists what's new and its SHA-256.

## FAQ

**Can I get banned for using it?**
Color Lab is purely cosmetic. It only changes how things look on your own screen, so it shouldn't count as cheating in any way, and it gives no advantage in the game.

**How do I change my real look without losing my custom one?**
In the garage, switch to **Real** (or pause Color Lab with ⏻), change your tank as normal, then switch back to **All** or turn ⏻ on again. Your Color Lab look is kept the whole time and is never sent to TankTrouble, so players without Color Lab only ever see your real look.

**Can other players see my look?**
Only players who also use Color Lab. They see your Color Lab look (you can switch this off in **Settings > Look sharing**). Everyone else sees your normal tank, unless you use the garage's **Real** mode, which changes your actual tank with items you own.

**Does it need my password or account?**
No. It never reads, keeps or sends your password, and there's no Color Lab account.

**Will I lose my looks when I update?**
No. Copy the new files over your old folder and your looks are kept. You can also back them up with **Settings > Export**.

**Something looks wrong or doesn't fit the tank. What should I do?**
Let me know on the [forum thread](https://tanktrouble.com/forum?id=475180) or open an [issue](https://github.com/wazzey/tanktrouble-color-lab/issues), ideally with a screenshot.

## Privacy

- Runs **only on tanktrouble.com**. It can't see other websites, tabs, history, cookies, passwords or downloads.
- Apart from TankTrouble, it only talks to **Color Lab's look server** (`ttcl-share.wazzey.workers.dev`, run by me on Cloudflare), for look sharing and to check for new versions. The update check only asks the server which version is newest (the server gets that from GitHub); nothing about you is sent.
- Browser permissions: **storage** (to save your looks) and **scripting** (so the toolbar button / Alt+C can open the panel).
- It reads TankTrouble's own game messages in the page to recolour tanks and track achievements. Your login passes through the game normally. Color Lab never reads, keeps or sends your password.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no account.**
- **Look sharing** stores, for each of your logged-in tanks with a Color Lab look: its **player ID** and **that look**, plus a scrambled key so only your copy of Color Lab can change it. To show other tanks' looks, your copy sends the player IDs of the tanks around you. No passwords, stats or browsing are sent, and the server keeps no record of who asked for which looks. So I can deal with abuse, the server also notes each shared tank's TankTrouble name (which anyone can see in the game) next to its look. To stop people sharing looks for other players' tanks, a look is only shared once you **prove the tank is yours**: Color Lab changes one thing on your real tank for about a second (one of the paints you already wear moved to another part, or one item taken off), the server reads the tank's public details on TankTrouble to see the change, and your tank is put straight back. You do this once per tank, and Color Lab asks first. Looks can be reported from a tank's profile. The server also only lets a copy of Color Lab (and an internet connection) share a few new tanks a day; for that it keeps a scrambled one-way code of your connection for a day, never next to a look. Switching off **Share my look** removes your looks from the server.
- **Online status:** every few minutes your copy tells the look server it's running: a scrambled code of your copy's key, its version, when it was first and last seen, which of your tanks are logged in, your TankTrouble server and whether you're idle. Other players only see you in the online list if you turn on **Show me as online**. It's deleted 60 days after your copy was last seen. **Go offline** (⚙ box or Settings) stops all of this; the server only notes once that you went offline on purpose. Full details are in `WHAT IT CHANGES AND PRIVACY.txt`.
- **Messages** you send from **Settings > Feedback & reports** (text and any screenshots) go to my Discord with your tank's name. The text and replies are kept so you can see them in Color Lab; screenshots aren't kept.
- Everything else it saves stays **on your own computer**:
  - your looks, presets and settings;
  - the usernames of tanks you give a custom look;
  - your achievement progress;
  - a backup copy in TankTrouble's site storage;
  - a picture cache.
- The zip includes `WHAT IT CHANGES AND PRIVACY.txt` with the full details.

### Removing everything

1. Optional: **Settings > Export** to keep a copy of your looks.
2. Switch off **Settings > Look sharing > Share my look**, so your looks are removed from the look server.
3. Remove the extension at `chrome://extensions`.
4. Clear tanktrouble.com's site data. Click the padlock next to the address, then go to **Site settings > Delete data**.

## Source code and builds

The files in this repository are the readable source of the current release. Release zips are built from them automatically by GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/release.yml`) when a version tag is pushed: `content.js` is minified with terser and packed with the other files, and the zip is attached to the release. Each build's SHA-256 is shown in the Actions run, so anyone can check a release came from this source.

To build it yourself: `npx terser@5.31.0 content.js -c -m -f ascii_only=true -o build/content.js`, then copy the other files next to it.
