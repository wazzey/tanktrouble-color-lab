TANKTROUBLE COLOR LAB - WHAT IT CHANGES AND PRIVACY
Not affiliated with TankTrouble. A fan-made, cosmetic-only extension.

Color Lab is cosmetic only. It gives no advantage in the game: no aim help, no
seeing through walls, no speed or reload changes. Everything below is visual,
and only YOU see it unless you choose to save a real change to your tank.


WHAT IT CHANGES ON YOUR SCREEN
- Tank colours: turret, base and treads of your tanks and of any tank you
  give a custom look.
- Paints and items: extra pattern paints and accessories (hats, glasses,
  capes, auras and so on), in their own boxes in the garage.
- Garage: adds folders (Original / Custom) and Color Lab boxes to the shelf,
  plus a search panel.
- Maps: the maze floor, walls, crates and decorations can use a map theme.
- Bullets: bullet colours can match the map, and multi-coloured bullets spin.
  The laser and aiming lines are recoloured so they stay visible on every map.
- Laika (the computer opponent): Laika's tank and Laika's dog can dress up to
  match the map.
- Achievements: an extra Achievements tab with hidden achievements that
  unlock Color Lab items and paints. They are separate from the game's own.
- A panel (Alt+C), a short tutorial and update notes.


WHAT OTHER PLAYERS SEE
The normal game. Color Lab looks are never sent to the game's server.
The only exception is the garage's "Real" mode (and the dice / "Find in Real"
buttons). These change your actual tank on your account, exactly like the
game's own garage, and only with items you already own.


WHAT IT CAN ACCESS
- Only tanktrouble.com. It does not run on any other website and cannot see
  your other tabs, history, cookies, passwords or downloads.
- Browser permissions it asks for: "storage" (to save your looks) and
  "scripting" (so the toolbar button / Alt+C can open the panel).
- It reads TankTrouble's own game messages in the page, so it can recolour
  tanks and notice kills, wins and pickups for achievements. Your login
  passes through the game the normal way. Color Lab does not read, keep
  or send your password.


WHAT IT STORES (only on your own computer)
- Your saved looks, presets, favourites and settings.
- For tanks you give a custom look: their in-game username, player ID and
  any nickname you type.
- Achievement progress: your kills, wins, streaks and which days you played.
  The time of day is checked for one achievement and is not saved.
- A backup copy of the above in TankTrouble's site storage in your browser,
  so your looks survive reinstalling the extension.
- A cache of pictures so the garage loads faster.

Nothing is collected by the author. There is no tracking, no analytics and
no account. Nothing about you is sent anywhere.

It only ever talks to TankTrouble itself: no other website is contacted.


DELETING EVERYTHING
1. Settings > Export first, if you want to keep a copy of your looks.
2. Remove the extension at chrome://extensions.
3. Clear tanktrouble.com's site data (click the padlock next to the address,
   then Site settings > Delete data). This removes the backup copy and
   picture cache.


STAY SAFE
Only install Color Lab from the official download:
https://github.com/wazzey/tanktrouble-color-lab/releases
Check the zip's SHA-256 matches the one listed there. A changed copy from
someone else could do things this one does not.
