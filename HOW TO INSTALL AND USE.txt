TANKTROUBLE COLOR LAB - v1.4
Made and run by wazzey. Not affiliated with TankTrouble.
Custom colours, paints, items and map themes for TankTrouble. Cosmetic only: you and other Color Lab users see them, everyone else sees the normal game.

Official download (the only one): https://github.com/wazzey/tanktrouble-color-lab/releases
Check the zip's SHA-256 matches the one listed there before installing.

INSTALL (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera)
1. Unzip. Keep the "TankTrouble-Color-Lab" folder - the browser runs it from there.
2. Open chrome://extensions (edge:// or brave://).
3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the "TankTrouble-Color-Lab" folder.
5. Pin it from the puzzle-piece icon, then open or refresh tanktrouble.com.

UPDATE (keeps everything)
Copy the new files over your old folder, press reload on the extension, then refresh TankTrouble.

USING IT
- Alt+C opens the panel. Alt+V refreshes your tank's look.
- Garage: "All" shows Color Lab boxes in folders (Original / Custom). "Real" is the game's own and saves to your account. All never changes your account.
- 🗺 Maze tab: 75 map themes (light or dark), themed bullets and crates, and Laika dressed for the map.
- 🏆 tab: hidden achievements - earn them in online rounds to unlock items and paints.
- Page themes (Maze tab): the page, buttons and lobby in a map's colours, plus Halloween, Christmas and Easter. Match the map, keep one, or a new one every day.
- New versions: Color Lab tells you when one is out (Settings > Help to check or switch off).
- Look sharing: other Color Lab users see your look, and you see theirs. Click a tank for its Real / Color Lab switch. Switch it off in Settings > Look sharing.
- ? buttons explain everything and replay the tutorial.

Back up your looks with Settings > Export.
Full details of what it changes and what it stores: "WHAT IT CHANGES AND PRIVACY.txt".
Feedback and bug reports: Settings > Feedback & reports, the forum thread or GitHub issues.
